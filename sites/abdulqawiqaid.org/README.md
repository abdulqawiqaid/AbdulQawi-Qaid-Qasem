# abdulqawiqaid.org — Official Blog

**Production website:** https://www.abdulqawiqaid.org/

This directory records the live Blogger publication and its supplied theme source. It does not deploy or control the live website.

## Purpose and editorial scope
The blog publishes professional and educational material in:
1. Digital health and health information systems.
2. Health education.
3. Healthcare quality and patient safety.
4. Health insurance and clinical auditing.
5. Research and health statistics.
6. Healthcare leadership and management.
7. Medical policies and healthcare job descriptions.

## Platform and ownership details
- Publishing platform: Blogger.
- Canonical public domain supplied by the project owner: `https://www.abdulqawiqaid.org/`.
- Arabic is the primary language of the supplied theme and public content.
- The live site is independently hosted and administered through Blogger; adding source files here does not change the live site.

## Operational safeguards
- Do not upload this theme to the live Blogger installation from this repository.
- Do not change the domain, DNS, redirects, AdSense, Analytics, consent settings, or Blogger widgets without explicit authorization.
- Any future theme remediation should occur on a separate branch, retain the original source, and include XML validation and a Blogger test-environment review before a production decision.
